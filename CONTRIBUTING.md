# Contributing

This guide gets better with real-world reports from clinicians. Contributions are welcome.

## Most wanted

- **Compatibility reports:** "EHR app X / MDM Y / VoIP platform Z works (or doesn't) on GrapheneOS with sandboxed Play, Pixel model N." Include the GrapheneOS release, the profile setup, and any workaround.
- **Corrections:** GrapheneOS menus, carrier features, and vendor BAAs change. Link a primary source.
- **State-specific notes:** address-confidentiality programs, board-listing privacy options, telehealth texting rules.
- **Templates:** translations of the patient policy and auto-replies.

## Ground rules

- **No PHI. Ever.** No screenshots with patient data, no real message examples.
- **Don't identify yourself, your employer, or your patients.** Say "a large academic health system using Intune," not the organization's name. Scrub screenshots of names, numbers, and tenant IDs.
- **Cite sources** for factual claims about GrapheneOS, HIPAA, carriers, or vendors. Prefer official docs.
- **Mark uncertainty.** "Worked for me on release X" is useful. "Definitely works everywhere" isn't.
- This is a practical guide, **not legal or compliance advice.** Keep the tone that way.

## Style

- Second person ("you"), plain language, short sections.
- Tables for comparisons, checklists for actions.
- Menu paths in **bold**, with "(may vary by release)" where appropriate.
