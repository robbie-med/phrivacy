# Quarterly review checklist

About 30 minutes, four times a year.

## Device
- [ ] GrapheneOS is up to date, and the device has rebooted recently
- [ ] Auditor attestation is still passing
- [ ] Apps updated in **every** profile (open each profile; stopped profiles don't update)
- [ ] Uninstall unused apps in each profile
- [ ] Review permissions: Network, Sensors, Location, Contact Scopes, Storage Scopes

## Separation
- [ ] No work or patient apps or accounts in Owner
- [ ] No patient contacts in Android contacts (either profile)
- [ ] No clinical images in any gallery. Empty the trash in Clinical.
- [ ] Personal cloud backups contain no PHI

## Numbers
- [ ] Search your **personal** number on people-search sites and messengers. Does it resolve to you?
- [ ] Search your **patient-facing** number. It should resolve to the practice only.
- [ ] Carrier port-out lock still active; account PIN unchanged and secret
- [ ] Patient-line office hours, auto-replies, and coverage still accurate

## Accounts
- [ ] Recovery codes still valid and stored off-phone
- [ ] No new SMS-based 2FA on important accounts
- [ ] Revoke old linked devices (Signal, email, patient platform)

## Laptop
- [ ] Work laptop: encryption on, OS updated, no personal apps or accounts crept in
- [ ] Review linked devices and sessions in the work vault and patient platform

## Backups
- [ ] Personal backup is recent. Do a test restore once a year.
- [ ] The duress PIN is still something you remember and won't enter by accident
