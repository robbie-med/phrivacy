# Template: Auto-replies & canned responses

Load these into your patient platform's saved replies. Keep them short. Always include emergency instructions in after-hours replies.

## After-hours auto-reply

> Thanks for your message to [PRACTICE NAME]. Our office is closed, and your message will be read on the next business day. If this is an emergency, call 911. For a mental health crisis, call or text 988.[ For urgent issues that can't wait, call [ON-CALL NUMBER].]

## Welcome / first message (new patient on the line)

> Hi, this is [PRACTICE NAME]. Save this number to reach our care team for non-urgent questions. Please don't use it for emergencies (call 911). Our communication policy: [LINK]. Reply STOP to opt out of texts.

## Out of office

> [Dr. NAME / Your doctor] is away until [DATE]. [COVERING CLINICIAN / Our team] is covering, and we'll respond within [TIMEFRAME]. For emergencies, call 911.

## Needs a visit

> Thanks for reaching out. This question needs a visit so we can examine you and give you the best answer. Please book here: [LINK] or call [FRONT DESK NUMBER]. If symptoms get worse, go to urgent care or call 911.

## Possibly urgent symptoms

> The symptoms you describe may need care right away. Please call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room now. Don't wait for a reply to this message.

## Sensitive information over text

> For your privacy, we'll send the details through the secure portal/message link: [LINK]. Please log in to view it.

## Redirect from a personal channel

(Send from the **patient line**, never from the personal account.)

> Hi [NAME], this is [Dr. NAME] from [PRACTICE NAME]. Please use this number for anything related to your care so our team can help you and keep your information in your record. I'm not able to respond to messages on personal accounts. Thank you for understanding.

## Restating boundaries (frequent after-hours messages)

> We want to make sure you get timely help. Messages sent after hours are read the next business day. For anything urgent, please call [ON-CALL NUMBER] or 911. Would you like to schedule a visit to go over these concerns together?

## Identity check before sharing PHI

> Before we share health information, please confirm the patient's full name and date of birth. If you're a family member or caregiver, we'll need a signed release on file.
