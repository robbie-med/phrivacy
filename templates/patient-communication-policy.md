### About texting
Regular text messages (SMS) are **not fully secure**. Other people could see them if your phone is lost or shared, or while messages are in transit.

- If you agree to texting, we'll keep medical details short. We may send a secure link for anything sensitive.
- You can choose to use only the patient portal or secure messaging instead.
- You can change your choice at any time.
