# Template: Voicemail greetings

Keep greetings under 30 seconds. Put emergency instructions **first**. Using the practice name instead of your own name is fine, and it keeps your name off the recording.

## Office hours (patient line)

> You've reached [PRACTICE NAME]. If this is an emergency, hang up and call 911. We're helping other patients right now. Please leave your name, date of birth, and a callback number, and we'll return your call [today / within one business day]. You can also message us through the patient portal. Thank you.

## After hours (patient line)

> You've reached [PRACTICE NAME]. Our office is closed. If this is an emergency, hang up and call 911. For a mental health crisis, call or text 988. [For urgent medical questions that can't wait until morning, call [ON-CALL / ANSWERING SERVICE NUMBER].] Otherwise, please leave a message or send a portal message, and we'll respond on the next business day.

## Vacation / coverage

> You've reached [PRACTICE NAME]. Dr. [NAME / "your doctor"] is out of the office until [DATE]. If this is an emergency, call 911. [Dr. COVERING / our team] is covering. Please leave a message or call [COVERAGE NUMBER], and we'll get back to you within [TIMEFRAME].

## Personal line (not for patients)

Use a generic greeting that **doesn't say your name or occupation**:

> You've reached [FIRST NAME ONLY, or just the number]. Leave a message.

Even better, use your carrier's default greeting. That way, if a patient ever gets the number, the greeting doesn't confirm who they reached.
